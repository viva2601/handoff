Q: What is the free checked baggage allowance?
A: Most economy fares include one checked bag up to 23 kg. Basic fares may include cabin baggage only.

Q: How early should I arrive at the airport?
A: Arrive 2 hours before domestic flights and 3 hours before international flights.

Q: Can I change the name on a ticket?
A: Minor spelling corrections are usually free. Changing to a different passenger is not allowed.

Q: Are infants allowed to fly?
A: Yes, infants aged 7 days to under 2 years can fly on an adult's lap for a reduced fare.

Q: What is the cancellation policy for flights?
A: Cancellations made more than 24 hours before departure are eligible for a refund minus a cancellation fee that depends on the fare type. Flexible and premium fares have lower fees or none. Non-refundable fares return only airport taxes. Refunds go back to the original payment method and typically take 7 to 10 working days.

Q: What happens if my flight is delayed?
A: For delays over 2 hours, airlines usually provide meals or refreshments. For delays over 6 hours, you may be offered a free rebooking or a full refund. If an overnight delay is caused by the airline, hotel accommodation and transfers are generally provided. Check the airline's notifications for the revised departure time.

Q: How many cabin bags can I carry?
A: Each passenger can carry one cabin bag up to 7 kg plus one small personal item such as a handbag or laptop bag. Cabin bag dimensions are typically limited to 55 x 35 x 25 cm. Items over the limit may need to be checked in for a fee.

Q: Can I choose my seat in advance?
A: Yes, seats can be selected in advance. Standard seats may be free on some fares, while extra-legroom, window and exit-row seats usually carry a fee. Exit-row seats have age and mobility restrictions.

Q: What documents do I need for an international flight?
A: You need a passport valid for at least six months beyond your travel dates, with enough blank pages for stamps. Depending on the destination, you may also need a visa, an electronic travel authorisation, or proof of onward travel. Some countries ask for proof of accommodation or sufficient funds. Check the entry requirements of your destination and any transit country well before travelling, because airlines can deny boarding if documents are missing. Carry printed or digital copies of your ticket and travel confirmation, and keep originals in your cabin bag rather than checked luggage.

Q: What items are not allowed in cabin or checked baggage?
A: Flammable items, explosives, compressed gases, and corrosive or toxic substances are prohibited in both cabin and checked baggage. Liquids in cabin baggage must be in containers of 100 ml or less, all placed in one transparent resealable bag. Sharp objects such as knives and scissors must go in checked baggage. Lithium batteries and power banks must be carried in the cabin and not checked in. Lighters are limited to one per person, carried on your person. If you are unsure about an item, check with the airline before your trip to avoid it being confiscated at security.

Q: How do I request special assistance or meals?
A: Special assistance such as wheelchair support, help for passengers with reduced mobility, or travelling with medical equipment should be requested from the airline at least 48 hours before departure. Special meals including vegetarian, vegan, child and religious meals can be requested up to 24 to 48 hours before the flight, depending on the airline. Requests made late may not be guaranteed. Confirm the request with the airline and mention it again at check-in.

Q: What is the policy for unaccompanied minors?
A: Children aged 5 to 11 can usually travel alone only through the airline's unaccompanied minor service, which carries an additional fee. A parent or guardian must complete a form, escort the child to the check-in counter, and provide the contact details of the person collecting them at the destination. Children aged 12 to 17 may travel as regular passengers or use the service as an option, depending on the airline. Not all routes or connecting itineraries support this service, so it should be arranged with the airline in advance.
